## Selection and source scope

Fifteen Region 9 Category 5 waterbody–pollutant pairs are represented. The release restores Tijuana River / dissolved oxygen and Tecolote Creek / pH, re-adds Carmel Valley Creek / dissolved oxygen after normalized matching resolved the `LPC-TWAS-3` ↔ `LPCTWAS3` formatting difference, and adds two additional fixed-criterion pH pairs: Tijuana River and Los Penasquitos Creek. Including Rainbow Creek nitrate, four of the fifteen pairs are non-dissolved-oxygen pairs.

Official pair IDs, 2026 decision IDs, final fact-sheet links, and final 2024 waterbody geometry are in `public/data/waterbodies.json`. `pipeline/out/appendix-b1-loe-audit.json` contains every Appendix B1 line of evidence, its program/dataset text, source reference IDs, date range, `n`, exceedances, include flag, criterion, station text/codes, and CEDEN comparison. Official counts sum only rows with Appendix B1 column AM = Y; excluded LOEs are retained in the audit but not added to official totals.

The field-result inventory contains 2,985 dissolved-oxygen rows, 4,561 pH rows, and 4,645 temperature rows (also 2,370 nitrate, 634 nitrite, and 1,659 selenium rows); pair files contain only the pollutant analyte for that pair. All CEDEN `Program`, `ParentProject`, and `Project` values were retained.

## Station matching and coverage

All 124 distinct station codes cited in the selected Appendix B1 LOEs were normalized by uppercasing and removing non-alphanumeric characters. This resolves punctuation-only variants such as `LPC-TWAS-3` / `LPCTWAS3` and `SDR-TWAS-1` / `SDRTWAS1`. CEDEN chemistry contains records for 119 of the 124 normalized codes. Five codes have no CEDEN chemistry or location match: `CC-SD8`, `SD8`, `RSW-003`, `RSW-006`, and `SD5`. The CEDEN location resource directly matched 61 codes; the chemistry files supplied coordinates for another 58, so 119 cited stations have mappable points. Unmatched codes remain logged in `ceden-source-reconciliation.json` and are not fabricated on the map.

### Calibration rule

The five exact-match pairs reproduce their included official LOE totals when the app includes numeric, unqualified water results with primary collection/results replicates, no QACode, and either `DataQuality=Passed` with `BatchVerification=VAC` or `DataQuality=Unknown data quality` with `BatchVerification=NR`. The same eligibility function is applied before and after the cutoff. The five matched LOE windows contain 331 results and 43 exceedances.

No non-detect/censored qualifiers occur in the five calibration windows. Because the app extract does not carry reporting-limit/method-detection-limit values needed to interpret censored results against each criterion, ND, `<`, DNQ, and other non-`=` qualifiers remain pending QA. Explicit rejected records, QC samples, invalid dates, and non-water matrices are removed before screening; unknown QA outside the two calibrated combinations, other QACodes, missing numeric results, and non-primary replicates remain pending.

## CEDEN field and continuous/sonde coverage

CEDEN chemistry and Augmentation were queried for dissolved oxygen, pH, and temperature field results across all program values. Temperature rows are inventoried but are not mixed into DO/pH pair files. The exact `sonde` search returned 13 catalog entries; six broader continuous/logger queries surfaced 24 candidate datasets, documented by title, URL, and notes in `continuous-source-audit.json`. Those candidates are primarily Delta/Suisun, Truckee, San Francisco Estuary, Van Damme coast, and Upper Mattole resources. The audit also checked DWR Hydstra stations in San Diego County: the single surface-water station at Pauma Valley is upstream of the selected Lower San Luis Rey segment, is not a cited LOE code, and has no period record in the queried layer. LLARRA and South-Central Coast drought-monitoring sites are outside these selected Region 9 reaches. No continuous/sonde candidate was merged into pair counts without an LOE station/source match.
