# Calibrated QA screening validation

**Dataset:** region9-r3-provisional-2026.4; **status:** provisional; **QA rule:** region9-calibrated-qa-v1. The data remains provisional and is not a regulatory determination.

## Calibration rule

The five exact-match pairs reproduce their included official LOE totals when the app includes numeric, unqualified water results with primary collection/results replicates, no QACode, and either `DataQuality=Passed` with `BatchVerification=VAC` or `DataQuality=Unknown data quality` with `BatchVerification=NR`. The same eligibility function is applied before and after the cutoff. The five matched LOE windows contain 331 results and 43 exceedances.

No non-detect/censored qualifiers occur in the five calibration windows. Because the app extract does not carry reporting-limit/method-detection-limit values needed to interpret censored results against each criterion, ND, `<`, DNQ, and other non-`=` qualifiers remain pending QA. Explicit rejected records, QC samples, invalid dates, and non-water matrices are removed before screening; unknown QA outside the two calibrated combinations, other QACodes, missing numeric results, and non-primary replicates remain pending.
